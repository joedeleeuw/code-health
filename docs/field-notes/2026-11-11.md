## Rust wrapper removed; Rust is a preset

Owner decision after the review: "aren't we deferring to tools? ah remove the wrapper" and "codify the rules for best ease of incorporation ... keep rules and integrate ease ... with corpora". `code-health` no longer runs Clippy or a Rust build. `presets/rust` holds `clippy.toml`, `lints.toml` and an ast-grep config over `code_health/config/rules/rust`. Proof: a project that copies the two files fails plain `cargo clippy` on its oversized functions.

This dissolves rows 2, 5, 6, 9 and 11 above (no wrapper to count per file, skip uncovered files, stop on a failed build, write `target/` or replace the user's settings). Row 1 becomes the preset's own thresholds in Clippy's terms. Row 3 is fixed for the `self`-dropping and return-type cases. Row 4 is `suppressed-limit-rust`. Row 7 no longer applies to Rust: there are no recorded Rust counts. Row 8 (copied functions) stays open. The cost: Rust has no "fail only on new findings"; a workspace fixes what it has or sets its own thresholds.

`suppressed-limit-rust` excuses a stated reason. Without one it would report 92 standing findings in monoframe (62 `too_many_arguments`, 30 `type_complexity`, most with reasons); with it, 9.

## Rust rules back in one default set

The split into default and opt-in rules judged a rule by how much more often it fires in agent-written than mature Rust. That is not the bar: a rule is judged by whether its findings are on its directive. `vague-name-rust`, `elif-chain-rust`, `dated-comment-rust` and `host-path-rust` are back in `rules/rust`, and `rules/rust-style` is gone. `vague-name-rust` reports 1.5 names per file (mostly `value`, `result`, `params`, `ctx`, `items`, `data`, `req`), which is what its directive bans.

`cargo lint` also denies `dead_code`, `unused_imports`, `unused_variables` and `unused_must_use`, which the removed Clippy wrapper counted and the first alias dropped.

Owner decision: `vague-name-rust` is default-off. It lives in `rules-off/rust` and runs through `presets/rust/sgconfig-off.yml`; the other three restored rules stay default.
